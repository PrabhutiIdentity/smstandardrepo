Answer

I updated the receipt flow so it matches your sales scenario better.

Now:
- `StockDetails` payment-history `Print` works from the row itself, even when the page is opened normally and not only after save.
- receipt cancel is now `receipt-wise`, not whole sale cancel.
- cancelled receipt keeps the same receipt number.
- cancelled receipt print shows `CANCELLED` in red and shows cancel date + cancel remark.
- after cancelling a receipt, the sale becomes payable again based on remaining valid receipts.
- `SalesReport` now keeps showing the latest receipt entry even if that receipt is cancelled, and `Print Receipt` stays available for cancelled receipts too.
